02. Problem Formulation

Grid Stress Points & The TOU Rebound Paradox

Why traditional unmanaged charging and static tariffs fail to protect distribution infrastructure

Uncontrolled Charging

  • Coincident Evening Peak: Commuters plug in Level 2 chargers between 17:00 and 21:00.
  • Severe Overload: Peak reaches 4.46 MW on a 2.38 MW rated transformer (+87.7%).
  • Voltage Sag: Downstream nodes drop to 0.951 p.u., violating ANSI C84.1.

The TOU Avalanche Peak

  • Timer Synchronization: 75% of residential users set vehicle timers for 23:00.
  • Secondary Spike: Massive 4.31 MW spike at midnight (+81.6% overload).
  • Ineffective Policy: Static pricing merely shifts the bottleneck without expanding capacity.

Thermal Degradation

  • Hot-Spot Spikes: Winding temperature surges to 189.4°C (Limit: 110°C).
  • Insulation Loss of Life: Aging acceleration reaches 74.2× normal.
  • Asset Replacement: $450,000 transformer fails in 2.8 years instead of 20 years.

03. Engineering Methodology

Mathematical Physics & Data Pipeline

Rigorous modeling of stochastic EV fleet telemetry, transformer thermodynamics, and radial power flow

1. EV Fleet Telemetry

  • Empirical Dataset: 3,500 sessions with battery capacities from 40 to 98 kWh.
  • Stochastic Arrival: Dual-peak Gaussian distribution (Home & Workplace).
  • Flexibility Index: Φ = t_dwell / t_min; determines load deferral and V2G windows.

2. IEEE C57.91 Thermal Model

  • Top-Oil Dynamics: dΘ_TO / dt = (Θ_TO_u - Θ_TO) / τ_TO.
  • Winding Hot-Spot: Θ_H = Θ_TO + ΔΘ_H_u.
  • Arrhenius Aging: F_AA = exp( 15000/383.15 - 15000/(Θ_H + 273.15) ).

3. LinDistFlow Feeder Model

  • 13-Bus Radial Feeder: 12.47 kV distribution network with realistic line impedances.
  • Voltage Sag: V_{i+1} = V_i - (R_i * P_i + X_i * Q_i) / V_i.
  • Joule Losses: Solves active power line dissipation at each hourly timestep.

05. Sustainable Mitigation & Roadmap

The Sustainable Engineering Solution

Tri-pillar architecture: Solar PV, BESS storage, and decentralized V2G grid dispatch

Solar PV + BESS Sizing

  • 800 kWp Solar Carport: Directly fuels daytime workplace charging sessions.
  • 1.2 MWh BESS Storage: Charges during peak solar (11:00-14:00), discharges 350 kW during evening peak (18:00-21:00).
  • Zero Solar Curtailment: Maximizes local renewable utilization.

V2G Bidirectional Fleet

  • ISO 15118 & SAE J3072: Enables automated vehicle-to-grid handshakes.
  • Peak Shaving: Parked vehicles inject 2.5 kW each during peak, earning utility credits.
  • Overnight Replenishment: Batteries recharge during clean off-peak wind hours.

Techno-Economic Payoff

  • $450,000 CapEx Saved: Substation transformer replacement avoided.
  • 20-Year Lifespan Preserved: Eliminates rapid thermal wear.
  • 1,336 Tons CO₂/Year: Direct carbon reduction per suburban substation.
